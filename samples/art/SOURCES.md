# Art references — provenance and licensing

These images are **references and placeholders**, not cleared assets.

They were downloaded from public image search results on 25 October 2026 to serve as:

- framing-scenario photographs (`stage-01.jpg`, `stage-02.png`, `stage-03.jpg`)
- visual references for the bag illustration (`bag-01.jpg` … `bag-04.jpg`)

## Licensing status

**Unverified.** The sources are third-party sites (Pinterest, Amazon product photography, a church
staging vendor). No licence was confirmed for reuse or redistribution.

That is acceptable while the project is local and unpublished. It is **not** acceptable for a public
GitHub repository, which submission requires.

## Before the repository goes public (6-ship)

Pick one:

1. Replace the stage photographs with known-licensed images (Unsplash, Pexels, or your own photos of
   a lectern from the back of the room). The bag art doesn't have this problem — it is drawn as SVG
   during the build, and these files are only references.
2. Keep the images out of git (add to `.gitignore`) and load them locally when recording.
